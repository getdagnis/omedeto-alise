# Roadmap

Current position: **phase 2 beginning**

---

## Phase 1 — v1 prototype (complete)

Goal: prove **interaction + vibe works** offline

- Soundboard interaction
- Character editing & slot logic
- Mobile-first layout
- Cyber-night Tokyo visual identity

Outcome: **user can feel characters alive**, sound combos work, basic UX is engaging

---

## Phase 2 — progression loop (current)

Goal: **introduce meaningful progression & collectability**

- Combo cards (3–6 sound recipes)
- Slot unlocks (initially 1 sound slot + 5 slots are locked)
- XP & goal panel
- Strong visual feedback for goal achievements, approaching targets
- Weekly featured combos
- Stabilize UI with **react-aria-starter primitives**

Desired effect: **user feels accomplishment**, wants to collect and share

---

## Phase 3 — profile & social (next)

Goal: **identity & social layer**

- Persistent user profiles
- Saved combos & visibility modes
- Social remixing: visit friends, propose combos
- Upvotes and basic leaderboard

Effect: **users feel community and recognition**

---

## Phase 4 — discovery & expansion

Goal: **dynamic world and motivation loops**

- New combos feed
- Trending / top combos
- Public profiles
- Event-driven weekly challenges

Effect: **users are drawn back, experience variety, goal-setting becomes natural**

---

## Phase 5 — multi-character & collection depth

Goal: **increase user attachment & strategic depth**

- Inventory / multi-character support
- Swap characters in profiles
- Character progression beyond single slot
- More advanced combo recipes

Effect: **users develop a personal identity in the platform**

---

## Phase 6 — cross-platform & polish

Goal: **shared logic across Web, Mobile, Desktop**

- React Native / Electron base
- Unified UI components
- Asset sync & cloud storage
- UI storytelling & subtle animations

Effect: **experience feels consistent, polished, and immersive everywhere**
