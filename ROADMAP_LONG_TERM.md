# Omedeto-Alise: Long-Term Vision & Roadmap

## 1. Economy & Growth (EARN / SHOP)

- **Currency System**: Integrated in-game budget for purchasing sounds, character upgrades, and items.
- **Earning Mechanics**:
  - Completing "Ultra Mode" challenges.
  - Social multipliers: Inviting friends and active communication.
  - Passive income: Friends earn money for you when they are active.
  - Live Notifications: Small, high-energy modals showing the source of incoming funds (e.g., "+50 Credits: Alise triggered a combo on Mark's board!").
- **The Shop**:
  - Dedicated `/shop` route and menu item.
  - Centralized marketplace for sounds (moved out of the character edit view).
  - Expansion packs: New character images, unique capabilities, and cosmetic features.

## 2. Gameplay Loop: Grow Your Squad

- **Character Scaling**:
  - **Offline Mode**: Access to all 6 base characters immediately.
  - **Online Mode**: Start with 1 character.
  - **Upgrade Path**: Unlock slots for more characters (1 -> 3 -> 6 -> 12).
  - **Social Integration**: Remaining slots on a user's board are filled with their friends' active characters.
- **Character Evolution**:
  - Level up characters through use.
  - Add new sounds, images, and features via the Shop.
  - Personalization: Write custom messages or micro-stories for each sound added to a character.

## 3. Social & Metadata

- **Featured Boards**: Points earned based on how high your character ranks on your friends' soundboards.
- **Social Interaction**:
  - Live chat with friends.
  - Profile engagement: Commenting and liking each other's characters and setups.
  - Global/Friend Rankings.
- **Shipping**: In-game item gifting and shipping mechanics.

## 4. Stories & Cyberpunk Tokyo

- **The Setting**: A Cyberpunk-era Tokyo where characters have distinct anime personalities and lives.
- **STORIES Section**:
  - Static lore and character bios initially.
  - Evolution into participating in weekly story creation.
  - Voting system: Participants earn significant rewards if their story contributions are voted highly by the community.
- **Visuals**: Future implementation of low-frame anime-style animations to bring the stories to life.

## 5. UI/UX Evolution

- **Dynamic HUD**: Bottom panel split between the "Ultra Mode" challenges (Left) and the live "Budget/Wallet" display (Right).
- **Expansion**: The "Ultra Mode Challenge" dashboard will evolve into a full-screen "Career" view tracking long-term goals.
- **Backend Migration**: Moving towards a Supabase-powered backend with secure login to persist all progression, currency, and social data.
